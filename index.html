ASHWICK

Tap a place. A key is a building you can hold. A town place is not.

The rules

THE KEY

The key is the token. It opens the building and the box behind it. You can rent a room. The renter does not get the box. If the key sells, the renter stays until the week ends.

THE ROOMS

A house has two rooms. An inn has beds. A shop has a floor and a back room. A shop can sell goods, list a token, or list an NFT. Swaps stay at the Trade Depot.

THE MAP

Gold buttons are places. Town places are not keys.

THE GREEN

A park. Benches. One lamp. Not a key. The Town Pub is the same. It belongs to everyone.
