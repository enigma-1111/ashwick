THE RULES

  1. The Green, the Town Pub, Sweep Market, the Trade Depot, The Count, and the Well are not for sale.
  2. A lamp key is a building. Inside it are rooms. A house can rent a room. An inn can rent a bed. A shop can sell goods, list a token, or list an NFT.
  3. Swaps happen at the Trade Depot, not in a house. The town takes a small cut.
  4. The renter gets the room or the sign. The renter does not get the box.
  5. If the key sells, the renter stays until the week ends.
  6. Hearth Row, Quiet, Chapel Lane, and Gate are drawn. They are not for sale until First Wick is used.
  7. No one is promised rent, visitors, or profit.
