THE RULES

  1. Ashwick starts as one street. Its name is First Wick.
  2. A lamp is a token. If you hold it, you hold the box behind it.
  3. The box can hold dollars, the city coin later, and stock tokens. What you leave in the box goes with the lamp.
  4. You set the price. Anyone can pay it and take the lamp. The page shows the box first.
  5. Most of that money goes to you. A small cut goes to the city.
  6. You can rent out the sign for a week. The renter does not get the box. If the lamp sells, they keep the sign until the week ends.
  7. Leave a lamp dark and someone can take it at the cheap floor price. You still get time to empty the box.
  8. No one is promised rent, visitors, or profit.

Later streets stay dark until this one is used. Hearth Row, Sweep, Quiet, Chapel Lane, Watch, Gate.
